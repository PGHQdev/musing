2. Description of Service

Musing is a browser extension that:

  • Replaces your new tab page with personalized historical quotes
  • Extracts themes from your AI conversations on supported platforms (Claude, ChatGPT, Google Gemini)
  • Matches quotes to your interests using local processing
  • Optionally explains quote choices (Smart Reasons) using an AI provider API key you supply

By default, all processing occurs locally on your device. If you enable the optional Smart Reasons feature with your own API key, conversation-derived text is sent directly from your browser to the AI provider you chose (Groq, Anthropic, or OpenAI).
